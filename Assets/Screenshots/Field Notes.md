## From plain text to a good read

- **Stay in the flow.** Open a file, drop it in, or follow a local link.
- **Keep the details.** Tables, code, and quotes feel right at home.
- **Make yourself comfortable.** Light by day. Dark after hours.

```sh
# Your next good read starts here.
open -a MarkyMark notes.md
```
